Forza Horizon 5

Forza Horizon finally comes to Japan. While delivering a visually stunning experience in quality mode it is limited to 30 FPS. You must play the game in performance mode to get 60 FPS. The Tokyo area is highly detailed and includes a large number of events to ensure you never run out of activities to complete. The arcade-style racing sets the bar excitement that still sets it apart from the sim racing of the regular Forza Series. There is still a massive number of cars to acquire throughout the experience that gives the events a lot of replayability. This is one of the best experiences XBOX has to offer.

Elden Ring

Elden Ring delivers a mixed experience on XBOX depending on what system you are playing on. The gameplay blends From Software’s signature Souls Like combat style with a massive open world. The open world contains a deep story that unfolds across brutal boss battles and area exploration. However this is somewhat hindered based on which console you play on. If you play on a XBOX One the game suffers visually with look and poor performance compared to the XBOX Series version that is beautiful and smooth. If you pick this title up make sure you are playing on a XBOX Series console.
