PLAYSTATION 5

Ragnarok God of War

This game builds on the story of the previous God of War released on the ps4 and re-released on the PS5. The plot is a deep emotional experience focusing on the struggles of Kratos and his son Atreus. The plot wraps up the Norse pantheon by triggering the prophecy of Ragnarok, culminating in a final battle with Oden to wrap up the plot. Combat is very fluid and is enhanced by the DuelSense controller’s haptic capabilities. It is a beautiful experience that plays at a smooth 60 FPS.

Like A Dragon: Infinite Wealth

In Infinite Wealth, the Like A Dragon series leaves Japan and takes the franchise to Hawaii. The game is a beautiful open world that is bright and vibrant compared to the earlier entries in the series. Unlike the previous Like A Dragon title this story is played from multiple angles with two protagonists. One story is lighthearted and fun while the other is an emotional roller coaster that takes you through a nostalgia-heavy wrap-up of Kazuma Kiryu’s storyline. The game runs at a near perfect 60 FPS with stunning visuals that are the best of the series by far. The story still holds to the previous Like a Dragon turn based fighting system, however it has been refined to make each battle feel more engaging. There is also a significant amount of side content that can provide hours of additional gameplay.
