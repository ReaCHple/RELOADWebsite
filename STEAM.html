Dragon Age: Origins

Dragon Age: Origins is likely one of the greatest and mostimmersive RPGs that offers meaningful choices and consequences. You play as a customizable character that was forced away from their home and must join the order of the Grey Wardens to fight againist an apocalyptic threat known as the Blight. Each choice starting from character creation has a major impact on the story, starting from your character's race, class, and background. Additionally, the game features a variety of interesting companions that may join you, each with their own story and abilities.

Dressmaker

DressMaker is a simple yet very attention catching game; I personally chose to take my time in games like this, so I love that there is no time limit when creating the dresses. I enjoy unlocking more fabrics and accessories as I progress through the game! The game is also quite forgiving when you make a mistake in the game, allowing you to recut or swap fabrics.
