Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate is one of the greatest party games ever made. It features 89 characters in its final roster, and supports up to 8 players. It features innovative new modes that can turn what would be a few matches into hours of fun with groups of any size. Each of its 89 characters has been crafted to be distinct and packed with references to their source material. It even shines as a competitive game, with players still hosting and playing in tournaments to this day, 8 years later.

Fire Emblem: Three Houses

Fire Emblem: Three Houses takes place in a medieval fantasy setting, with the story centering on a monastery holding the balance between 3 kingdoms at an uneasy peace. You play as a teacher in charge of teaching students from all 3 kingdoms about all there is to know about military strategy. The game play is deep, requiring careful management of up to 15 characters on the field at any given time. It requires careful thought, and even features permanent death of characters, so mistakes really matter.
